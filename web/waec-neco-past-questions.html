Why WAEC and NECO English aren't treated the same

WAEC's objective English paper tests Lexis and Structure and Oral English; NECO's version folds in a Literature section that WAEC's doesn't have. Exam Coach keeps these as separate, syllabus-matched question banks rather than one generic "English" pool, so what you practice actually matches what you'll sit.
