Why school-specific matters

Post-UTME screening isn't standardized the way JAMB is — every university sets its own English, Mathematics, and General Knowledge questions, and the difficulty and style vary noticeably between schools. Exam Coach curates a dedicated question bank per school for those core subjects, rather than giving everyone the same generic pool. UNILAG and UNILORIN candidates get a single fixed practice test matching each school's actual format; the other six schools use a JAMB-style picker — English compulsory, then three more subjects of your choice, pulling from your target school's own bank where available and the shared JAMB bank for the rest.

Stuck on a question? Snap it.

Photograph any question and get a step-by-step explanation in seconds — useful for General Knowledge questions that don't map neatly onto a single subject. You can also find and challenge other candidates targeting the same school, live, right inside the app.
